## The problem

Space missions are governed by long, interlocking regulations and standards such as the ECSS series. Turning a mission brief into a complete set of engineering requirements is slow, manual work, and missing an implication early is expensive later.

## What I built

- **Requirements derivation.** AI-assisted functionality that reads space mission briefs alongside ECSS regulations and standards and derives the engineering requirements they imply.
- **Structured, traceable outputs.** ReqIF 1.2 exports and attribute-gap reports, so extracted requirements arrive in a format engineers can review, exchange and trace.
- **Production backend.** On-premises LLM services behind a TypeScript, NestJS, Prisma and PostgreSQL backend.
- **Verification.** CI/CD pipelines with Docker-based verification and automated schema, round-trip and integration testing.

## Results

- **98%** of the engineering implications in a mission brief and its applicable standards captured by the derivation.
- My work contributed to a team-wide **40%** reduction in documentation time.
