## The idea

Most of what an organisation knows is said out loud in meetings and then lost. Syncnapse is organisational AI memory: it captures that knowledge and keeps it owned by the company.

## What it does

- **Agents in the meeting.** Users spawn, customise and invite voice agents into virtual meetings. The agents join the discussion, raise opinions and interact with participants.
- **Agents that act.** They can take actions such as creating documents, scheduling events and recording information.
- **Memory with guard rails.** Conversations and meeting insights become manageable memory through human-approved AI proposals, permission layers and multi-agent synchronisation.
